Open items from the replay-pins work:

  than ~10 s, whose reply was lost, retransmits its prepare; a fresh round decides abort if no object still shows the
  update. Store state stays correct and repair catches up the stores, but the client is told the wrong outcome.

  (docs/safety transaction-recovery F2 / R-N2): there is no failure path. Pre-existing, made slightly worse by the
  marker now running last, so a failed earlier action also loses the ErrorTree marks and UnackedCommits entries.

  after the replay-pins branch.
