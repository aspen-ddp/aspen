Bare allocateDataObject() (no initial content) leaves 0-byte slices in slicing pools (ReedSolomon and
AppendingReedSolomon). The allocating-object pre-pass in TransactionBuilder.buildTranaction adds
DataBuffer.Empty plus only a RefcountUpdate requirement (no DataUpdate), so RequirementsApplyer never
applies the encoded empty update. Full reads then fail with a non-NotRestorable exception,
readAppendPosition fails with CorruptedObject and appends abort with AppendMismatch. Fix direction: add a
DataUpdate for allocating objects, or have stores apply the update for allocating objects. Until fixed,
AppendingReedSolomon objects must be allocated with initial content (even empty content that is encoded).

QUIC tests seem to fail randomly. Likely due to load. The test suite needs to pass correctly under load.
Investigate the causes of these failures and see what can be done to improve reliability. Note simply
increasing timeouts/linger and the like is generally not acceptable as it can dramatically increase the
duration of the regression. Some increases for reliability are probably allowable but they must be
relatively small in the common case. If the test can somehow detect that it is running under load and
increase its timeouts/linger only under those circumstances, that might be reasonable.
