Implement DataUpdateOperation.Append correctly for slicing IDAs
  - Currently rejected at every layer: TransactionBuilder.append throws AppendNotYetSupported,
    RequirementsChecker fails the requirement with UnsupportedOperation, RequirementsApplyer throws
  - The old store-local append was silently incorrect under ReedSolomon: each store appended raw
    update bytes to its own encoded fragment, producing data that does not reconstruct
  - A correct implementation must encode the appended data through the pool's IDA and hand each
    store its own fragment
    - Note that the encoding strategy will usually (maybe always) require that the backends overwrite
      part of the tail of the data they currently hold. It will not be a simple operation of appending
      the new data to the old data when an erasure-coded IDA is used.
