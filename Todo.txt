Recovered commit votes of forgotten transactions (closed 2026-11-08). Changes that leave no trace (RefcountUpdates,
KeyValue deletes without a required revision) are recorded in each pool's TracelessCommits tree for stores that miss
them, and a recovered commit vote applies a commit found there. Repair reconciles exactly, and late prepares past the
status cache horizon vote TransactionTooOld. See docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-traceless-commits-design.md.

QUIC applies the idle timeout (`QuicNet`: three heartbeats unless `QuicConfig.idleTimeout` is set) during the
handshake too. In the tests a CPU-starved handshake outlasted 1.5 s and the dial closed, so on a host starved for

Fixed (2026-11-06): JVM SIGSEGV in librocksdbjni at `rocksdb::WriteBatchInternal::Count`, from
BufferedConsistentRocksDB.commitOnPool, seen twice in full `sbt test` runs. BufferedConsistentRocksDB.close() released
the database and the next WriteBatch as soon as no operation was inside begin/endOperation, but a commit still queued
on the pool, or one past its write, then read the freed nextBatch in its chaining step. close() now waits until no
commit is queued or in progress, and is idempotent. Covered by the two close tests in BufferedConsistentRocksDBSuite.
The bug predates the host refactor; the store executors only changed how often closes overlap commits.
