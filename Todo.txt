PRIORITY 3: systemic loss protection
------------------------------------

(The correlated false "store lost" design item remains in Backlog.txt.)

3.3  CRL-W6  A loaded state is judged never to fit against the current limit, not the grown one. CONFIRMED, S.
     StoreLoader.neverFits compares against admission.limitBytes + T. A crl_save.log state that would fit after
     growth (up to crl-max-streams) is declared lost (crlSaveFileLost) and rebuilt. Fix: judge "never" against the
     limit at the growth cap, and let anything below it wait offline while requesting growth.


PRIORITY 4: liveness (hangs, stalls, livelocks)
-----------------------------------------------

4.1  RB-W7  A pool short of live peers holds a host rebuild slot for days, or indefinitely. CONFIRMED, S.
     queryPeers (client.currentAllocations) has no deadline and runs in every sweep round. The walk is sequential at
     30 s per timed-out read and aborts only after MaxFailedObjects (10,000, about 83 h). Two such stores fill both
     of a host's slots. Fix: bound the peer query, and abort a pass early when peers are below threshold.

4.2  RB-W8  abandonedRebuilds leaks when an abandoned rebuild does not end in RebuildCancelled. CONFIRMED, S.
     A rebuild past finish's latch re-check, or one with another cause already latched, keeps its entry. Later
     rebuilds of that store on the host cannot be cancelled by abandonUnlisted, which breaks B18. Fix: clear the
     entry in release().

4.3  RP-W3  A store marked again between passes never reaches a proof or parking. PLAUSIBLE, S.
     errorDeferralKey(key, value.revision) resets both the lock-deferral and the stale counts whenever the entry is
     rewritten, and every mark rewrites it. A store stale on a hot key is re-marked every pass and stays stale. Fix:
     keep the counts across revisions of the same entry key, and reset only the `only` narrowing.


PRIORITY 5: performance and hygiene
-----------------------------------

5.1  R-W5 + P-W6  Status-cache relay correctness. CONFIRMED, S each. Fix R-W5 first, or both together.
     - R-W5: resolved(true, record = false) still sets txStatus = Committed, and the post-proof prepare saves it.
       After a restart that resolution is restored as ordinary: it seeds the host cache with a non-finalized
       Committed and loses notOwedHere. The relay then resolves a forgotten T non-late, re-running finalization and
       the marker; with an entry naming another incarnation, the store applies T with no data. Fix: save Unresolved
       for a resolution learned from the tree.
     - P-W6: Store.handleTransactionMessage addresses the relay's TxResolved(msg.to, msg.from, ...) to the relaying
       store itself, so a remote recovery driver never learns the outcome from it (slower takeover). Fix: send it to
       msg.from and keep the local delivery. Fixing this before R-W5 spreads R-W5's bogus relay to remote drivers.

5.2  Q-W6  A re-applied traceless update quarantines an object that already holds it. CONFIRMED, S.
     Affects a RefcountUpdate alone, and delete-only KV updates, on a recovered non-late re-apply or an apply
     through an UnackedCommits entry. appliedBy finds no trace, so an ordinary crash quarantines the object; TKVL
     deletes and refcount decrements are common. Fix: in the re-apply check only, treat refcount == newRefcount (and
     the keys already gone) as applied.

5.3  RP-W4  A quarantine of a deleted object never clears. CONFIRMED, S.
     Covers the quarantine-side trigger in transaction-requirements.md 8.5 too.
     StoreRepairer.verifyObject does not treat InvalidObject(provesDeletion) as verified, so verification retries
     forever. The transaction's CRL state, admission bytes and stub stay pinned until reload (expireStubs /
     quarantinedConclusions), with a warning every minute. Applies whether the deletion came before or after the
     quarantine. Fix: treat a proven deletion as verified.

5.4  R-W6  A finalization action whose execute() throws orphans the marker before it. CONFIRMED, S.
     Finalizer calls execute() unguarded. A user action that throws synchronously fails the finalizer after the
     marker has scheduled, and that orphan marker then writes marks for every store, every retry (up to every 60 s).
     Fix: catch each execute() throw as that action's failure.

5.5  R-W3  UnackedCommits lookups pile up as untimed reads during a metadata outage. CONFIRMED, M.
     retryUnackedLookups re-issues every 5 s without cancelling the old lookup, and Host.unackedLookup reads through
     the untimed client: about N*D/5 retransmitting reads after an outage of D seconds with N waiting Txs. Fix: use
     a timed read (withReadTimeout), or re-issue only when the earlier lookup has finished.

5.6  RB-W9  A finished copy whose flip did not promote it is stranded when its entry leaves the device. CONFIRMED, S.
     After Superseded or a permanently failed flip, the copy is not in offlineStores, so the Deleted Stores pass
     never retires it before a host restart. Meanwhile every transfer of the store onto that device refuses ("store
     path already exists").

5.7  RB-W10  An out-of-space flush in finish is not classified. CONFIRMED, S.
     The pass fails generically, with no REBUILD OUT OF SPACE alert, and repeats with backoff.

5.8  CRL-W7  An expiring stub's charge is released before its deletion is queued. CONFIRMED, S.
     Frontend.dropAppliedStub calls releaseStub() before crl.deleteAppliedStub, breaking the release-after-deletion
     rule Lemma C (1) relies on. Up to 41 uncovered bytes per stub. Fix: swap the two calls.
